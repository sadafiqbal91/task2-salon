Who we are

Beauty that feels like you.

Velvet Rose began with a simple idea: a salon should be a place you look forward to, not just a place you need to go. We listen first, then we create.

Our team blends classic technique with modern style, using gentle products and taking the time to get every detail right.

  • Free consultation with every service
  • Experienced, friendly stylists
  • Hygienic tools and a clean, calm space
